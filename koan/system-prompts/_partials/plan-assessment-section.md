### Plan Assessment

Exactly three bullets of plain markdown — not wrapped in backticks or a code block — in this format:

- **Complexity:** S|M|L
- **Risk:** Low|Medium|High
- **Comment:** one clause

Complexity reflects the amount of work; risk reflects what breaks if the plan turns out to be wrong — blast radius, reversibility, data loss. Keep the `Comment` to a single clause naming the main driver. No other bullets, no extra lines.
